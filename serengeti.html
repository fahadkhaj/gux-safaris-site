Serengeti National Park

The endless plains where the Great Migration roams

Discover the Wonders of Serengeti National Park

Experience the wild beauty of the Serengeti like never before. Our carefully designed tours take you deep into the heart of Tanzania’s most celebrated national park, where endless golden savannahs meet an abundance of wildlife. From the thundering herds of the Great Migration to the stealth of predators on the hunt, every moment promises an unforgettable adventure.

Whether you’re enjoying a sunrise game drive, floating over the plains in a hot air balloon, or unwinding at a luxury lodge, each Serengeti tour is tailored to give you the perfect balance of excitement, comfort, and discovery.

Advance Facilities

Your Serengeti adventure combines comfort and convenience, ensuring you enjoy the wilderness with peace of mind. Facilities include:

  • Parking at safari camps
  • Pick-up & drop-off services from Arusha or Kilimanjaro Airport
  • Modern camp facilities with power and clean water
  • Evening entertainment: Maasai dances, traditional music, and campfire storytelling
  • Guided visits to the five most iconic Serengeti locations
  • Comfortable motorhome and luxury tented camp options
  • Daily meals with vegetarian and special diet options

Tour Plan

Arrive at Kilimanjaro or Arusha Airport. Meet your safari guide and enjoy a scenic drive or short flight to Serengeti National Park. Upon arrival, check into your lodge or tented camp and unwind with a welcome dinner under the stars.

  • Airport pick-up and transfer to Serengeti
  • Check-in at lodge or luxury camp
  • Evening welcome dinner and safari briefing

Start your adventure with a full day of game drives across the Serengeti plains. Spot the Big Five, migrating herds, and breathtaking landscapes. Break for a picnic lunch in the wild and finish the day with a magical sunset.

  • Morning and afternoon game drives
  • Chance to witness the Great Migration (seasonal)
  • Picnic lunch in the savannah
  • Sunset views with refreshments

Begin the day with an optional sunrise hot air balloon safari followed by a bush breakfast. Later, immerse yourself in Maasai culture with a guided visit to a traditional village.

  • Optional sunrise hot air balloon safari
  • Bush breakfast in the wild
  • Visit to Maasai village and cultural exchange

Venture into more secluded parts of the Serengeti with your guide, ideal for photographers and birdwatchers. In the evening, enjoy a traditional campfire with storytelling or local music performances.

  • Exclusive off-road game drives
  • Wildlife photography and birdwatching
  • Campfire dinner and cultural entertainment

Conclude your Serengeti journey with an early morning game drive. After breakfast, check out and return to Arusha or Kilimanjaro for your departure, taking with you memories of a lifetime.

  • Optional sunrise game drive
  • Breakfast at camp or lodge
  • Return transfer to Arusha or Kilimanjaro Airport
