Ngorongoro Crater

Africa's "Garden of Eden" – a breathtaking volcanic caldera teeming with wildlife

Explore the Magic of Ngorongoro Crater

Experience the wild beauty of Ngorongoro Crater like never before. Our carefully designed tours take you deep into the heart of Tanzania's most famous volcanic caldera, where a breathtaking landscape meets an abundance of wildlife. From the dense populations of lions, elephants, and rare black rhinos to the vast herds of wildebeest and zebra, every moment promises an unforgettable adventure.

Whether you're enjoying a sunrise game drive on the crater floor, visiting traditional Maasai villages nearby, or relaxing at a luxury lodge overlooking the spectacular crater rim, each Ngorongoro tour is tailored to give you the perfect balance of excitement, comfort, and cultural discovery.

Advance Facilities

Your Ngorongoro adventure combines comfort and convenience, ensuring you enjoy the wilderness with peace of mind. Facilities include:

  • Parking at safari camps and lodges
  • Pick-up & drop-off services from Arusha or Kilimanjaro Airport
  • Modern camp and lodge facilities with power and clean water
  • Evening entertainment: Maasai dances, traditional music, and campfire storytelling
  • Guided visits to the most iconic Ngorongoro locations, including the crater floor
  • Comfortable safari vehicles and luxury tented camp options
  • Daily meals with vegetarian and special diet options

Tour Plan

Embark on an unforgettable journey through the spectacular Ngorongoro Conservation Area with our specially designed safari itinerary. Each day brings a new adventure—from close wildlife encounters on the crater floor to cultural visits with the Maasai community—ensuring your safari is both thrilling and deeply memorable.

Arrive at Kilimanjaro or Arusha Airport, meet your guide, and transfer to a lodge near the Ngorongoro Crater rim. After check-in, relax and enjoy the stunning views of the highlands. In the evening, enjoy a welcome dinner.

  • Airport pick-up and road transfer
  • Check-in at Ngorongoro lodge
  • Welcome dinner and crater-view sunset

After an early breakfast, descend 600 meters into the Ngorongoro Crater for a full-day game drive. Explore the crater floor and spot lions, elephants, buffalos, hippos, and possibly black rhinos. Enjoy a picnic lunch at a scenic spot by the hippo pool.

  • Full-day crater game drive
  • Visit hippo pool and Lerai Forest
  • Picnic lunch inside the crater

Start your final morning with a guided nature walk along the crater rim or an optional visit to a nearby Maasai village. After breakfast, check out and return to Arusha or Kilimanjaro Airport for your onward journey.

  • Crater rim nature walk (optional)
  • Maasai cultural visit
  • Return transfer to Arusha or airport
