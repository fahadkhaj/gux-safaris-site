Discover the Wonders of Ruaha National Park

South-Central Tanzania

Journey into the heart of Ruaha’s incredible wildlife...

Experience the untamed wilderness of Ruaha National Park, Tanzania’s hidden gem and largest protected area. Our carefully crafted tours take you deep into the heart of this remote and rugged landscape, where vast plains and ancient baobab trees are home to diverse wildlife. From majestic herds of elephants to stealthy predators on the prowl, every moment promises a truly authentic safari adventure.

Whether you’re exploring on thrilling game drives, embarking on guided walking safaris, or relaxing at a secluded luxury camp, each Ruaha tour is tailored to offer the perfect blend of excitement, comfort, and discovery.

Ruaha National Park Advanced Facilities

Your Ruaha adventure blends the raw beauty of the wilderness with comfort and convenience, so you can focus on experiencing nature fully. Facilities include:

  • Secure parking at safari camps
  • Pick-up & drop-off services from Mbeya or Dar es Salaam airports
  • Modern camp amenities featuring reliable power and clean water
  • Evening entertainment with traditional tribal dances, live music, and storytelling around the campfire
  • Expert-guided safaris through Ruaha’s five most breathtaking wildlife hotspots
  • Choice of comfortable motorhome or luxury tented camps for a restful stay
  • Daily meals catering to vegetarian and special dietary requirements

Tour Plan

Set out on an unforgettable journey through the wild heart of Ruaha National Park with our carefully crafted safari itinerary. Each day offers new adventures—from close wildlife encounters to immersive cultural experiences—ensuring your safari is thrilling, enriching, and truly memorable.

Arrive at Mbeya or Dar es Salaam Airport, where your experienced guide will welcome you. Enjoy a scenic drive through the beautiful Tanzanian countryside to Ruaha National Park. Upon arrival, check in to your lodge or tented camp nestled within the park’s wilderness. Unwind with a refreshing drink and enjoy a traditional dinner while soaking in the sounds of the African bush.

  • Meet and greet at Mbeya or Dar es Salaam Airport
  • Scenic transfer to Ruaha National Park
  • Check-in at eco-friendly lodge or luxury tented camp
  • Welcome dinner featuring local flavors

Wake up early for your first full day in Ruaha, one of Tanzania’s largest and most unspoiled parks. Enjoy morning and afternoon game drives in search of Ruaha’s abundant wildlife, including large elephant herds, elusive leopards, lions, and rare wild dogs. Keep an eye out for the spectacular birdlife, including the colorful lilac-breasted roller and the majestic African fish eagle. Break for a bush picnic overlooking the Great Ruaha River, a vital water source for the park’s animals.

  • Morning and afternoon game drives in diverse habitats
  • Wildlife sightings: elephants, lions, leopards, African wild dogs
  • Birdwatching with over 570 species recorded in the park
  • Bush picnic lunch by the Great Ruaha River

Experience a guided walking safari led by your expert tracker, exploring the park’s unique flora and fauna up close. Learn about medicinal plants, animal tracks, and the fragile ecosystem of Ruaha. In the afternoon, visit a nearby local village to meet the Wapogoro or Hehe people, learning about their traditions, crafts, and way of life. Return to camp for a relaxing evening with a cultural storytelling session around the campfire.

  • Guided walking safari to discover smaller wildlife and plants
  • Cultural visit to a local Wapogoro or Hehe village
  • Traditional storytelling and music around the campfire

Explore remote corners of Ruaha known for their stunning landscapes and exceptional wildlife density. Today is perfect for birdwatchers and photographers, with opportunities to spot rare species like the Pels fishing owl or the ruaha red-billed hornbill. Optional boat safaris on the Great Ruaha River are available for a different perspective on the park’s ecosystem. End your day with a sunset game drive and a gourmet dinner back at camp.

  • Game drives to secluded and photogenic areas
  • Birdwatching highlights including rare and endemic species
  • Optional boat safari on the Great Ruaha River
  • Sunset game drive followed by a gourmet camp dinner

Enjoy a final early morning game drive to soak in the sights and sounds of Ruaha one last time. After breakfast, check out from your lodge and transfer back to Mbeya or Dar es Salaam Airport for your onward journey, carrying unforgettable memories of Tanzania’s wild heart.

  • Early morning game drive for last wildlife sightings
  • Breakfast at camp or lodge
  • Transfer to Mbeya or Dar es Salaam Airport for departure
