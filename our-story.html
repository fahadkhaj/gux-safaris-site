Our Story

Built by People Who Grew Up Here

GUX Safaris didn't start in a boardroom. It started on the roads out of Arusha — the same roads our guides have been driving since long before "GUX" was a name on a logo.

Tanzania doesn't need another safari company that treats the Serengeti like a checklist item. It needs people who know which track floods first in the long rains, which campsite actually has the best sundowner view over Ngorongoro, and which chef in Zanzibar makes the octopus curry worth the detour. That's the gap GUX Safaris was built to close — local knowledge, used properly, instead of a brochure written by someone who's never smelled the bush after rain.

Why "GUX"

We wanted a name that didn't try to sound like every other "Tanzania Safari [Something]" out there. GUX is short, it's ours, and it leaves room for the country itself to do the talking — the Serengeti, Kilimanjaro, Zanzibar's reefs. We're the guide, not the show.

"We don't sell trips. We build the trip you'd plan yourself if you'd grown up here."

What We Actually Believe

No Two Itineraries Alike

We build routes around your time and budget, not a fixed package we're trying to fill seats on.

Guides Who Live Here

Every guide is Tanzanian, trained, and driving roads they've known for years — not a script.

Low-Impact by Default

Smaller groups, community-linked camps where we can, and parks left the way we found them.

Straight Answers

If a route, season, or camp isn't right for you, we'll say so before you pay for it, not after.

Where We Operate

From Arusha as our home base, GUX Safaris runs the full Northern Circuit — Serengeti, Ngorongoro, Tarangire, Lake Manyara, Kilimanjaro and Meru — plus the wilder southern and western parks like Ruaha, Nyerere, and Mahale for travelers who want fewer vehicles and more silence. Add on Zanzibar for the back half of a trip, and you've got the itineraries we build most often.

We're a small team, growing carefully. That's on purpose — every guest who books with GUX Safaris still gets a real person on WhatsApp, not a call center.
